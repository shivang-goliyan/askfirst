Is this thing being sensible? — 30 cases, about 20 minutes

This is a student project. It is not a medical device, nothing in it is approved for anything, and nobody will ever be treated on the strength of it.

You are marking us, not the other way round.

There are no right answers on this sheet and nothing here is a test of you. We built something that decides when it should shut up and ask a human instead of guessing — and we have no idea whether its decisions look sensible to someone who has actually seen patients. That is the only thing we are asking.

For each case you get: what was written on the record, what our thing decided, and the questions it says to ask next. Then two ticks:

  1. Given only what was written down, was that a reasonable call? (It either answered, or refused and passed it to a person.)
  2. Is the first question it suggests the one you would have asked?

Things that will help us most:

  • Ticking "no" — a no with three words after it is worth more to us than twenty yeses.
  • Skipping anything you are unsure about. A guess is worse than a gap.
  • Stopping early. Ten cases is genuinely useful. Five is useful. Do not do thirty out of politeness.

Two of these are cases where our thing got it wrong. We are not telling you which. Finding them is the most useful thing that could happen to this project.

You can tick these in the page. It remembers your answers if you close the tab, and the Copy my answers button at the bottom writes the whole reply for you — paste it straight into a message. Nothing is uploaded anywhere; it never leaves your phone until you paste it.

If you would rather not use the page at all, a message like 1 y/y · 2 n/y too thin · 3 skip or a voice note is just as good.

The true diagnosis is deliberately not shown anywhere, so nothing nudges your answer.
